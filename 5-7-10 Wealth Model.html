Each year has 26 fortnights. Equity growth is the annual rate divided by 26 per fortnight, and each contribution lands after that fortnight's growth. HYSA interest is credited monthly, so a Year 3 transfer follows exactly 36 months of interest.

DCA amounts are in today's dollars. With a yearly raise they step up at the start of each year, and the amount after the step-down age rises the same way.

Cash tiers move at the end of the crash year. A tier deploys only what the HYSA still holds.

With prices left on trend, alpha is only the gap between the equity return and the HYSA rate on the cash that moved. Turn on the drawdown option to also model buying at the lows. These are projections from assumed returns, not a forecast.
