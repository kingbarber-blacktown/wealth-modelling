The simulator remains responsible for calculations.

Your question and a summary of your current settings/results are sent to Google Gemini via the Vercel backend. Avoid including identifying information. Check Google's current Gemini API terms before submitting sensitive data. AI output can be wrong and is not personal financial or tax advice.

Historical returns and currency: each preset uses the longest official annualised total-return series located, with reconstructed or back-tested history labelled where applicable. MSCI Australia, World and ACWI use gross-return series in AUD from 31 Dec 1987; MSCI Japan uses a net-return AUD series from May 1994. Nasdaq-100 and S&P 500 use USD-based long-run references. For USD-based presets, the model adds an editable estimated annual FX drift and combines index volatility with an editable FX-volatility component, assuming zero correlation. This is a practical approximation, not a full historical AUD return series; S&P 500 currency drift is especially uncertain over its pre-1969 history. Source periods and gross/net treatment differ, so figures are not perfectly comparable. Values are dated snapshots, not a live feed.

Scenario method: 300 reproducible Monte Carlo paths apply annual log-normal shocks around the selected historical CAGR; each simulated year’s return is applied over its fortnightly cash-flow steps. The selected CAGR anchors the central/median path, not a guaranteed expected return. This is not a bootstrap of historical returns and does not fully capture fat tails, changing regimes, cross-index correlations or future FX volatility. Percentile ranges are model outputs, not confidence intervals.

Tax and costs: estimated annual fund fees, brokerage per buy, tax on reinvested distributions, after-tax HYSA interest, and CGT on an assumed sale are included. The hidden starting cost base is assumed equal to the current portfolio value, so no pre-existing unrealised gain/loss is recognised. Distribution tax and foreign withholding are simplified; the HYSA tax treatment is also approximate. Bid–ask spreads, platform/FX costs, Australian franking credits, individual offsets, loss carry-forwards and parcel-by-parcel CGT timing are not modelled. The CGT discount is assumed to apply to all unrealised gains. This is an approximation, not tax advice.
